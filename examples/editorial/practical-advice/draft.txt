The moment a meeting starts is the worst time to discover your microphone is muted.

Starting an instant call with untested audio can consume the opening minutes of the meeting for every attendee.

• Check your camera and microphone before starting an instant SmartMeet meeting.

A quick pre-start device check is a practical habit for business teams who host instant meetings on SmartMeet.
